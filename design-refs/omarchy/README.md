# Omarchy wallpapers — candidates for the TV

Source: `/usr/share/omarchy/themes/<theme>/backgrounds/` on this machine
(23 themes, 74 wallpapers). Omarchy is MIT; the wallpaper images themselves
are third-party, bundled by the theme authors.

`contact-sheet.jpg` is all of them, in one image.

- **16:9** (green badge) — fits the TV's 1920x1080 without cropping.
- **crop** (amber badge) — a 3:2 or taller image; the launcher crops it to
  fill, so the top and bottom are lost. Fine for a background, but the
  subject may be cut.

The TV's panel is 1920x1080 and the launcher decodes the wallpaper at 1920
wide, so a 1920x1080 source is pixel-perfect.

To use one on the TV: resize to 1920x1080 and put it in
`/sdcard/Pictures/Slideshow/` — the launcher's slideshow picks it up from
there on its own.
